**Pick a style that fits this specific target's industry and brand tone — do NOT default to the same pattern across companies.** Four distinct styles are available; draw from ALL of them, not just one.

**Style A — Question-led (punchy, curious)**
- "Catching every call at {company}?"

**Style B — Observation-led (industry-hooked, lowercase-OK)**
- "Noticed {company} stays busy"
- "{company}'s rush hours"
- "peak season at {company}"
- "showings + phones at {company}"  (real estate)
- "chairside notes at {company}"    (dental / medical)

- "service calls at {company}"       (HVAC / home services)

**Style C — Short + curious (under 35 chars)**
- "Quick idea for {company}"
- "A thought on {company}"
- "{company} + two small tools"
- "Two ideas for {company}"

**Style D — Topic-paired (phones + conversations)**

**Tie-breaker rules**:
- If the target's industry has a distinctive activity (service calls, showings, test drives, consults, chairside), **prefer Style B with that industry hook** — it feels most personal.
- Otherwise rotate to Style A (question) or Style C (short).
- Avoid using the same exact phrase every time. The two weakest (most over-used) phrases to **avoid defaulting to**: "Catching every call + every conversation at {company}" and "Two tools for {company}'s phones + follow-ups" — only use these if Style B doesn't fit and no better option exists.
